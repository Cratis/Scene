## colors

Establish the color tokens - their roles and their values in every mode, in the theme - from the sources. Where two colors are nearly the same (for example two greys), keep one and name the other in the summary as a conflict, with where each is used if the sources show it.

## type-and-spacing

Establish the type, spacing, radius and shadow tokens (type, space, radius, shadow, and font for families) - each token role with its description, and its value in the theme - from the sources. Where the sources use two values for the same purpose (for example 6px and 8px corners), keep one and name the other in the summary as a conflict.

## components

Describe the components the sources show - buttons, inputs, cards, chips - as components with variants, each part using a token that exists in the design system. Do not add a component the sources give no sign of.

## words

Establish the voice and the naming in the design system from the sources: the tone the product's text is written in, and the terms it uses for its own things - the label text, headings and messages in the code, and the team's notes. Each word is one short sentence or term. Say in the summary what the sources do not show.

## running-app

Look at the captures of the running application first: its colors, type, corners and spacing as they appear in light and in dark. What the captures show is the truth about how the product looks; the files say how it is built. Where they disagree, say so in the summary.

## revise

Change the design system or its default theme as the person asked. Keep everything else as it is. If a change would break a component that uses a token you remove or rename, change the component too, and say so.
